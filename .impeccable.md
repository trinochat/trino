## Design Context

### Users
Trino is a private messaging app for people with mixed levels of technical
experience. They want secure direct and group conversations without having to
understand the transport, key exchange, or network internals during normal use.

### Brand Personality
Operational, clandestine, and calm. Trino should feel like a trustworthy modern
communications console: technically capable and distinctive, but never cryptic
for decoration's sake.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, terminal-inspired, and influenced by Hacknet. Keep the phosphor-green
identity, scanline texture, compact density, and precise monospace details.
Balance them with neutral surfaces, clear labels, familiar navigation, restrained
glow, and selective cyan/amber/red status accents. Avoid generic neon dashboards,
glassy cards, fake command-line interactions, and interfaces where every element
has the same visual intensity.

### Design Principles
1. Make messaging obvious; reveal network and cryptographic detail progressively.
2. Use phosphor green for identity and active state, not for every piece of text.
3. Separate navigation, conversation context, and utilities into clear regions.
4. Treat contacts and groups as parallel conversation types with consistent rows,
   previews, timestamps, unread states, and avatar behavior.
5. Preserve compact desktop efficiency while keeping touch targets and mobile
   navigation comfortable.
